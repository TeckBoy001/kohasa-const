About Kohasa Engineering

Nigeria's Oil & Gas Engineering Specialists

Kohasa Engineering Company Limited is a wholly indigenous Nigerian company specialising in the design and construction management of onshore and offshore oil and gas production facilities.

With extensive experience across all engineering disciplines, we deliver innovative and reliable solutions — from conceptual engineering and FEED to upgrades, repairs, and revamping of swamp, onshore, and offshore facilities.

Incorporated in 2003 and headquartered at Trans-Amadi Industrial Layout, Port Harcourt, our seasoned professionals are dedicated to cutting-edge solutions that meet the highest standards of safety, efficiency, and sustainability.
